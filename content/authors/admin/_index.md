Yichao Jin (Cary) is a PhD Candidate at UT Dallas. His Job Market Paper (JMP) bridges neural value integration with discrete choice models, introducing the **NICA Framework** (Neuro-computational Inference for Causal Agency). By utilizing structural estimation on wait-time parameters, his research quantifies waiting time as a **"Behavioral Tax"** (yielding a robust kappa=0.225), moving beyond traditional discounting models. His work pioneers the development of **Perspective-Aware AI (PAI)** and **Behavioral Digital Twins** (e.g., TRIBE v2), optimizing real-world policy and institutional trust in digitalized environments.
